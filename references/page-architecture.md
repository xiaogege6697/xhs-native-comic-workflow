# Page Architecture

## Page Count

- 5 pages: one simple misconception.
- 6 pages: standard education path.
- 7 pages: at least two classifications or one exception.
- 8 pages: evidence, professional distinction, reversal, and action checklist.
- More than 8 pages: split into a series instead of shrinking text.

## Cognitive Roles

| Role | Function | Visual Anchor | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| cover | stop | conflict object, protagonist, key promise | teaching everything on cover |
| scene | relate | message, bill, event, ordinary room | abstract lecture |
| evidence | observe | chat, bill, transfer, timeline | empty screenshots |
| framework | classify | folders, table, whiteboard, flow | more than 4 categories |
| reversal | reverse | mistaken pile vs true factor | gender blame, shock-only drama |
| action | act | checklist, reply script, next step | vague encouragement |

## Text Budget

- L1 title: 6-16 Chinese characters preferred.
- L2 conclusion: 6-18 Chinese characters preferred.
- L3 dialogue: max 2 bubbles per page, each under 20 Chinese characters.
- L4 labels: 2-6 Chinese characters each.
- If the page needs more words, split the page. Do not shrink text.

## Cover Requirements

The cover must include:

- the protagonist
- the conflict party or conflict message
- one concrete object such as a phone, bill, contract, transfer card, report, or checklist
- one strong title
- one value promise

Bad cover: “法律科普：恋爱转账问题解析”.
Good cover: “分手后，他让我退钱？” + “先看钱的性质”.

## Final Page Requirements

The final page must work as a saved checklist. It should include one of:

- four-column classification table
- reply script
- evidence checklist
- escalation rule
- decision tree

Do not end with only “关注我，下期见”.
