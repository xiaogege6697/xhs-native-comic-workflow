# Legal Comic Rules

## Scope First

For every legal comic, establish:

- jurisdiction
- current date
- relationship between parties
- timeline
- claims from each side
- disputed issue
- available and missing evidence
- legal relationships
- burden of proof
- exceptions
- next lawful step

## Legal Voice

Use conditional conclusions:

- “通常……但要结合……”
- “关键不在A，而在能否证明B。”
- “这只是判断线索，不是单独定案标准。”
- “可能存在多种性质，应逐笔区分。”

Avoid:

- “肯定赢”
- “一定不用还”
- “只要这样就行”
- fake article numbers
- fake case numbers
- fabricated official citations
- deleting records
- hiding assets
- inducing illegal evidence collection
- gender-hostile framing

## Legal Visual Evidence

Use documents only as illustrative evidence types:

- chat record
- transfer note
- bill
- contract
- IOU
- timeline
- witness/event note

Never present fictional screenshots as real case material. Do not include real names, phone numbers, ID numbers, card numbers, addresses, or QR codes.

## Breakup Money Example Framework

For “男女朋友分手后，男方让我退钱，我该不该还？”:

Core line:

```text
不是看他要多少，是看每笔钱是什么。
```

Minimum classifications:

- 赠与
- 借款
- 共同消费
- 结婚给付

Evidence logic:

- Borrowing: look for borrowing agreement plus payment.
- Gift/ordinary affection: special numbers and holidays are clues, not final answers.
- Common consumption: ask who consumed, who paid, and what was agreed.
- Marriage-purpose payment: separately assess purpose, registration, common life, local custom, and other facts.

Recommended final action:

```text
请逐笔说明性质和依据。
```
